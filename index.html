Requires a Mac (Apple silicon recommended, macOS 13 or newer) and a Claude account that includes Claude Code (Pro or Max). Listening and speech-to-text run on your machine; only the text of what you say goes to Claude.

What he does

Hey Jarvis

Wake word, speech-to-text, and a real conversation — he keeps listening after he answers, so follow-ups just work.

Actually useful

Gmail, Calendar, Google Drive, Spotify, texts, reminders, timers, web search — he reads things back and asks before sending anything.

A holographic HUD

A on-screen display that glows while he listens, spins while he thinks, and shows panels, schedules and drafts as he talks.

Mavis

A second personality who shows up on screen to tease, react, and riff — not just a voice, a little bit of company.

Your phone, too

A page on your phone becomes a second mic, keyboard and speaker for the same Jarvis, same memory, same connectors. With Tailscale it works from anywhere.

He remembers

A daily log and a running list of your bits and callbacks, handed back to him at the start of every conversation. Say "remember this" and it's filed instantly.

Type instead of talk

Didn't catch what you said, or just don't want to talk out loud? Type a line in the terminal — same Jarvis, no wake word needed.

Try saying

  • Good morning. Brief me.
  • What's on my calendar tomorrow?
  • Email Mom that I'll be late.
  • Play some jazz on Spotify.
  • Google who played guitar on Kind of Blue.
  • Set a timer for twenty minutes.
  • Open my Gmail and read the newest one.
  • Remind me to call Dan at seven.

Setup

  1. Easiest: paste the one-line command above into Terminal and skip to step 3. Or download the zip and unzip it somewhere permanent, like your home folder.
  2. Open Terminal, type bash (with a space), drag setup.sh into the window, press Return. It asks your name and a few details, installs what it needs (about 1.5 GB of speech models, one time) and triggers the macOS permission prompts: click Allow.
  3. If Claude Code opens, log in with your claude.ai account, then type /exit. On claude.ai, under Settings › Connectors, connect Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar and Spotify once.
  4. Double-click Jarvis.command and say "Hey Jarvis." He introduces himself the first time.

Full details, every setting, and troubleshooting are in the README inside the download.
