JARVIS

Your own Iron Man assistant, on your own Mac.

An always-listening voice assistant powered by Claude. Say “Hey Jarvis” and he answers, remembers, and gets things done.

Download for Mac

Free · about 10 minutes to set up · macOS 13 or newer

What you need

A Mac (Apple silicon recommended) on macOS 13+ A Claude account with Claude Code (Pro or Max) About 10 minutes

What he does

Hey Jarvis

A wake word that works from across the room. Keep talking for follow-ups, or say it mid-sentence to cut him off.

Actually useful

Connects to Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Spotify, plus reminders, timers and the web. Anything irreversible waits for your spoken “yes.”

Holographic HUD

A glowing on-screen display that shows what he’s doing, with a face that moves when he talks.

Meet Mavis

A second personality who drops in to tease, argue, and keep things from getting boring.

Phone page

Open the same Jarvis, with the same memory, from your phone on your Wi‑Fi, or from anywhere with Tailscale.

Type instead of talk

In a library or on a call? Type in his window. No wake word needed.

Try saying

Hey Jarvis, what’s on my calendar today? Set a timer for twenty minutes Remind me to call Mom at five Play something mellow on Spotify Read me my unread emails Never mind Start over Jarvis, shut down

Set up in four steps

  1. Unzip and run setup. Unzip the download somewhere permanent. In Terminal, type bash (with a space), drag setup.sh into the window, and press Return. It asks a few questions, installs what it needs, and triggers the macOS permission prompts. Click Allow on each. The first run downloads about 1.5 GB of speech models.
  2. Log in to Claude. If Claude Code opens, sign in with your claude.ai account, then type /exit.
  3. Connect your apps. On claude.ai, go to Settings › Connectors and connect Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar and Spotify. One time only.
  4. Say hello. Double-click Jarvis.command, wait for “Jarvis online,” and say “Hey Jarvis.” He introduces himself and tells you what he can do.

Privacy

Listening for the wake word and turning your voice into text both happen on your Mac. Only the text of what you say goes to Claude. Your settings and keys stay in a file on your Mac and nowhere else.
